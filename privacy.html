Privacy & Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CampusCraft collects, uses, protects, and manages information when you visit our website or use our products and services.

Introduction

Welcome to CampusCraft. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website and use our services.

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment details when you place an order, create an account, request a quotation, or contact us.

Your information may be used to process orders, manage customer accounts, respond to inquiries, prepare quotations, provide customer support, and communicate important updates regarding our products and services.

We may also use collected information for analytics, website improvements, service development, and improving the overall customer experience.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

We may work with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website, processing payments, delivering products, providing analytics, or supporting other business operations.

These service providers may only receive information necessary to perform their services and are expected to handle such information responsibly and securely.

Your Rights

You may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information that we hold about you, subject to applicable legal requirements.

You may also choose to opt out of promotional or marketing communications from CampusCraft at any time.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, understand website usage, remember preferences, and analyze website traffic.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

CampusCraft may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, business practices, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page. We encourage visitors to review this Privacy Policy from time to time.
