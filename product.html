Customization Guide

From idea to final kit, follow these simple steps to create fully customized schoolwear and teamwear with Campus Craft.

  1. 01

    Share Your Requirements

    Tell us about your school or team, including sizes, quantities, colors, and any existing branding or design references.

  2. 02

    Get a Tailored Quote

    We review your details and send a clear, itemized quote with pricing, timelines, and customization options.

  3. 03

    Approve the Design

    Our team creates mockups with your logo, colors, and text. You review and approve, or request revisions until it’s perfect.

  4. 04

    Production & Delivery

    Once approved, we move to production and deliver your customized order on time, ready for your school or team.
